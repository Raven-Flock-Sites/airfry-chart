Pro Tips for Perfect Results

Preheat Always

Give your air fryer 2–3 minutes to preheat. Cold start = uneven cooking.

Don't Crowd the Basket

Single layer is best. Overcrowding steams food instead of crisping it. Shake halfway.

Light Oil Spray

A light mist of oil (avocado or olive) makes the biggest difference for crispiness.

Check Early

Every air fryer runs differently. Start checking 2–3 minutes before the listed time.

Quick Wins

Crispy Wings

Pat dry, season generously, 400°F for 22–26 min. Shake twice. Sauce after cooking.

Perfect Salmon

380–400°F for 8–12 min. Skin side down. No flip needed. Internal 145°F.

Frozen Fries

400°F for 12–18 min. Shake every 5 minutes. Better than the oven every time.
