Crispy Chicken Wings

400°F • 20–26 minutes • Serves 2–3

  1. Pat wings completely dry and season generously with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  2. Preheat air fryer to 400°F for 3 minutes.
  3. Cook in a single layer, shaking the basket every 6–8 minutes.
  4. Check for crispiness at 20 minutes. Sauce after cooking if desired.

Perfect Salmon Fillets

390°F • 8–12 minutes • Serves 2

  1. Pat salmon dry. Brush lightly with oil and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Place skin-side down in the preheated basket.
  3. Cook at 390°F without flipping until internal temperature reaches 145°F.

Crispy Frozen Fries

400°F • 12–18 minutes

  1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Add frozen fries in a single layer (do not thaw).
  3. Shake the basket every 5 minutes until golden and crisp.

Garlic Butter Steak Bites

400°F • 6–10 minutes

  1. Cut steak into 1-inch cubes and pat dry.
  2. Toss with a little oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Air fry at 400°F, shaking halfway.
  4. Toss hot steak bites with melted garlic butter before serving.

Roasted Broccoli

375°F • 8–12 minutes

  1. Toss broccoli florets with oil, salt, and garlic powder.
  2. Air fry at 375–400°F, shaking halfway, until edges are crispy.

Honey Garlic Shrimp

400°F • 5–8 minutes

  1. Toss shrimp with a little oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Air fry at 400°F until pink and cooked through.
  3. Toss with a quick honey-garlic sauce (honey, soy, garlic, butter) right after cooking.

Crispy Chickpeas

400°F • 12–18 minutes

  1. Drain and dry canned chickpeas thoroughly.
  2. Toss with oil and spices (paprika, garlic powder, salt).
  3. Air fry at 400°F, shaking every 5 minutes, until crunchy.

Pork Chops

400°F • 12–16 minutes

  1. Season pork chops with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Brush with oil.
  2. Air fry at 380–400°F, flipping halfway.
  3. Cook to 145°F internal and rest 3 minutes.
