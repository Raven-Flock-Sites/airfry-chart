Essential Tips

Always Preheat

Run the air fryer empty for 2–3 minutes. Cold starts lead to uneven cooking.

Don't Crowd the Basket

Single layer is best. Overcrowding steams food instead of crisping it. Cook in batches if needed.

Light Oil Spray

A light mist of oil (avocado or olive) dramatically improves crispiness without making food greasy.

Shake or Flip Halfway

Most foods benefit from a shake or flip at the midpoint for even browning.

Check Early

Every air fryer is different. Start checking 2–3 minutes before the listed time.

Pat Food Dry

Moisture is the enemy of crispiness. Pat meats and veggies dry before seasoning and cooking.

Common Questions

Do I need to preheat?

Yes, for best results. Most recipes assume a preheated air fryer.

Can I cook frozen food from frozen?

Yes. Most frozen items (fries, nuggets, etc.) go straight in — no thawing needed.

How do I clean my air fryer?

Let it cool. Soak the basket and tray in warm soapy water. Wipe the inside with a damp cloth. Avoid abrasive scrubbers on nonstick surfaces. A paste of baking soda and water helps with stuck-on bits.

Why is my food soggy?

Usually overcrowding or not drying the food first. Also make sure you're not adding too much oil or sauce before cooking.

What internal temperatures should I aim for?

Chicken: 165°F • Pork: 145°F • Beef steaks: 145°F (medium) • Fish: 145°F. Always use a thermometer.

Temperature Conversion

Most charts use Fahrenheit. Quick reference:

  • 350°F = 175°C
  • 375°F = 190°C
  • 380°F = 193°C
  • 400°F = 200°C
