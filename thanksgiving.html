Air Fryer Turkey Breast

350°F • 40–55 minutes • Serves 4–6

  1. Pat a 2–3 lb turkey breast dry and season generously with salt, pepper, sage, and garlic.
  2. Brush lightly with oil or melted butter.
  3. Air fry at 350°F, flipping halfway, until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  4. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

Crispy Brussels Sprouts

375°F • 12–18 minutes

  1. Halve Brussels sprouts and toss with oil, salt, and a little maple syrup or balsamic.
  2. Air fry at 375°F, shaking halfway, until caramelized and crisp.

Sweet Potato Cubes

380–400°F • 15–20 minutes

  1. Peel and cube sweet potatoes. Toss with oil, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Air fry at 380–400°F, shaking twice, until tender and caramelized.

Air Fryer Stuffing Balls

350°F • 12–15 minutes

  1. Form leftover or prepared stuffing into golf-ball size portions.
  2. Lightly spray with oil and air fry at 350°F until golden and heated through.

Green Bean Almondine

375°F • 8–12 minutes

  1. Toss green beans with oil, salt, and garlic powder.
  2. Air fry at 375°F, shaking halfway.
  3. Top with toasted sliced almonds and a squeeze of lemon before serving.

Cranberry Turkey Meatballs

380°F • 10–14 minutes

  1. Mix ground turkey with breadcrumbs, egg, salt, pepper, and a spoonful of cranberry sauce.
  2. Form into meatballs and air fry at 380°F until cooked through (165°F).

Roasted Carrots with Honey

380°F • 12–15 minutes

  1. Toss carrot sticks with oil, salt, and a drizzle of honey.
  2. Air fry at 380°F, shaking halfway, until tender and glazed.

Air Fryer Mashed Potato Cakes

375°F • 10–12 minutes

  1. Form leftover mashed potatoes into patties. Dust lightly with flour or breadcrumbs.
  2. Spray with oil and air fry at 375°F until golden and crisp on both sides.
